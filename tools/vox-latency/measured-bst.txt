  The two 'fixed' rows are the control's own values: they are what this
  compiler spends per invocation before any obligation is considered.
  On the base compiler they are a Digest.file over the compiler and
  solver binaries, and a flat 100 ms sleep in the solver teardown.  Both

  THE -vox-dump-vc BOUNDARY, AND WHY NO ROW ABOVE IS DERIVED FROM IT:
    dump - no-verify                   0.0047
  -vox-dump-vc builds and formats every obligation but exits before
  emitting bytecode -- it writes no .cmo, and -vox-no-verify does.  So
  this difference is (construction + formatting) MINUS (bytecode
  emission), which on a module this size are the same size, so it lands
  at the noise floor with an arbitrary sign.  Reading it as the cost of
  building obligations understates that cost by a whole .cmo emission,
  and charging the rest to the row above it overstates that by the same.
  The table above therefore takes its boundary at -vox-no-verify, which
  emits bytecode exactly as the warm and cold rows do.
