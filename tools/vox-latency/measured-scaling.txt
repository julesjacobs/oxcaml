  So the (cold - warm) row is not solving.  It carries a
  per-invocation charge worth roughly 111 obligations of solving,
  and reading the whole row as solver time overstates it by that
  much on any module smaller than that.

  -vox-no-verify builds no obligations at all, so OBLIGATION

  separate construction row to it: that counts construction twice.
  The second fit's dependent variable includes the second SMT emission
  a cold obligation pays, so its slope is an UPPER BOUND on solving
