Design synopsis (proposed, not a checked proof):

Use finite syntactic value/environment certificates. Prove capture-avoiding
substitution preserves certificates. The original proposal uses induction on
fuel for evaluation; the user has since requested a partial, fuel-free evaluator
with ghost typing evidence and unreachable_ () in impossible runtime branches.

Erase well-formedness bookkeeping from D.typed into R.typed: drop n, context_wf,
mono_wf, arguments_wf and scheme_wf, but retain exact scheme arity checks and
let RHS weakening. Prove D.typed implies R.typed by structural recursion on
the original derivation. Soundness of the more permissive R judgement must be
proved; it is not assumed.

judgement = J_value of value * mono | J_environment of value * context
certificate = Leaf | Capture of context * typing * certificate
                   | Extend of certificate * certificate

Leaf proves True:Boolean or Empty:Empty_context. Capture proves a closure has
an arrow type from a body derivation and captured-environment certificate.
Recursive closure evidence contains no self certificate. Its body is typed
under argument, self-arrow, captured context. At application, insert the existing
closure certificate into the body's environment certificate.
Extend proves Bind(v,env):Binding(Forall(k,a),g) from certificates for v:a and
env:g. These are finite symbolic certificates, not implicit universal booleans.

Finite substitution representation: {front: arguments; tail: index}.
If front has m elements, index i<m maps to front[i]; m+j maps to Parameter(tail+j).
Lift through k binders to preserve the first k parameters and shift substituted
types. Acting on a scheme Forall(k,a) acts on a using the lifted substitution.
Opening uses {front=args;tail=Z}; weakening uses {front=No_arguments;tail=k}.

Required algebra: action commutes with type shifts and opening schemes;
action on weakened contexts commutes with lifting. Prove pointwise index
lemmas structurally, then type/context lemmas structurally. A split_index
helper can return None with present k i, or Some j with i=add k j.
No function extensionality or general substitution-composition theorem needed.

Transform typing derivations structurally: substitute argument/result types;
lift through let-generalized binders in the RHS derivation. Prove this preserves
R.typed using lookup substitution, arguments length, action/open commutation,
and context/lift/weakening commutation.

Transport certificates structurally: Leaf stays Leaf; Capture substitutes its
captured context and body derivation and transports its environment certificate;
Extend transports its head under lift k substitution and tail under the original
substitution. Runtime values are unchanged by all these operations.

Typed lookup uses environment certificates and instantiates the head certificate
at index zero, recurring into the tail at successors. This does not evaluate the
value again or invoke inference.

The direct partial interpreter should replace the fuel-indexed soundness theorem
with a recursive refinement contract. Total ghost substitution and certificate
operations remain necessary. This adaptation has not yet been independently
fully implemented or checked.
